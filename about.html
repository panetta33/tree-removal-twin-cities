About Tree Removal Twin Cities

The fastest way to get connected with a top tree removal company in the Twin Cities metro.

What We Do

Tree Removal Twin Cities is a local referral service. We connect homeowners in Minneapolis, St. Paul, and the surrounding metro with local tree removal companies — established Twin Cities businesses with real addresses, real customer reviews, and the equipment to do tree work professionally.

When you call (952) 260-9151 or submit a quote request, your information is routed to a tree service partner that covers your city. They contact you directly — typically within an hour during business hours — and provide a free, written estimate. You don't pay us anything. You only pay the tree service if you choose to hire them.

Why a Network Instead of Just Calling a Tree Service Directly?

  • You save time. One call or one form, and the right local company comes to you. No three-quotes-three-callbacks routine.
  • You get a starting point. We've already filtered out obvious storm chasers and out-of-state fly-by-nighters. We still recommend you verify your chosen provider's license, insurance, and references directly — but the pre-screen narrows the field.
  • You don't pay extra. The tree service quotes you the same price they'd quote any customer. There's no markup for going through us — we're paid by the tree service, not by you.
  • You're free to say no. The estimate is free. If you don't like the quote, you owe nothing.

What to Look For When Hiring a Tree Service

Tree work is high-risk. Before letting any tree service start work on your property, we strongly recommend you verify:

  • A current certificate of insurance — call the listed agent to confirm it's active
  • An active Minnesota business license and a permanent local address
  • A written, itemized estimate signed before work begins
  • Recent reviews from Twin Cities homeowners (Google, Nextdoor, Angi)
  • References from local jobs they've done in the last 6 months
Why this matters: if an uninsured tree service drops a tree on your house, your homeowner's insurance pays — and your premiums go up. Verifying license and insurance up front is the single most important step in protecting yourself.
