About Tree Removal Twin Cities

The fastest way to get connected with a top tree removal company in the Twin Cities metro.

What We Do

Tree Removal Twin Cities is a local lead-routing service. We connect homeowners in Minneapolis, St. Paul, and the surrounding metro with vetted tree removal companies — the kind of local pros who carry real insurance, run safe crews, and show up when they say they will.

When you call (952) 260-9151 or submit a quote request, your information is routed to a tree service partner that covers your city. They contact you directly — typically within an hour during business hours — and provide a free, written estimate. You don't pay us anything. You only pay the tree service if you choose to hire them.

Why a Network Instead of Just Calling a Tree Service Directly?

  • You save time. One call or one form, and the right local company comes to you. No three-quotes-three-callbacks routine.
  • You get vetted pros. We've already filtered out the unlicensed storm chasers and shady operators. Every company in our network is fully licensed and insured.
  • You don't pay extra. The tree service quotes you the same price they'd quote any customer. There's no markup for going through us — we're paid by the tree service, not by you.
  • You're free to say no. The estimate is free. If you don't like the quote, you owe nothing.

What We Look For in Partner Companies

Not every tree service in the Twin Cities is welcome in our network. To get matched with our homeowner leads, a company has to demonstrate:

  • An active Minnesota business license and a permanent local address
  • General liability insurance (typically $1M–$2M+) and workers' comp coverage
  • ISA-certified or experienced arborists on staff
  • A track record of responsive customer service and fair pricing
  • Positive reviews from real Twin Cities homeowners
  • Willingness to provide written estimates and proof of insurance on request
Why this matters: if an unlicensed tree service drops a tree on your house, your homeowner's insurance pays — and your premiums go up. By only working with insured partners, we make sure that risk stays where it belongs.

Storm damage, fallen trees, and hanging limbs don't wait for business hours. Our partner network includes companies that dispatch 24/7 across the Twin Cities metro. When you call (952) 260-9151 after-hours, the call routes to a partner that can roll a crew immediately.

For more on emergency response, see our 24/7 emergency service page.
