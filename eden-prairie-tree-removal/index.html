Specific Tree Issues in Eden Prairie

Three local pressures dominate tree work in Eden Prairie:

Emerald Ash Borer (EAB). Eden Prairie has been an EAB quarantine zone for years, and the city has been actively removing infested public ash trees. Most private ash trees in the city are now either dead, infested, or on borrowed time. If you have an ash on your property, expect it to need to come down within the next few years — and waiting until it's fully dead makes the removal more dangerous and more expensive.

Oak wilt. Eden Prairie sits in a high-risk oak wilt area. Infected red oaks can die in a single growing season, and the disease spreads to neighboring oaks through connected roots and beetle activity. Removing infected oaks promptly — and correctly disposing of the wood — is critical to protecting healthy oaks nearby. We follow MN DNR oak wilt protocols.

Storm damage. Eden Prairie's mature canopy is beautiful, but big old trees take big hits during summer storms. We've removed countless silver maples, cottonwoods, and box elders dropped or compromised by wind and ice. After storms, call us early — appointment slots fill fast.

Eden Prairie tree ordinance: the City of Eden Prairie regulates the removal of certain "significant trees" on commercial and development sites, and you may need a permit if you're removing trees on a constructable lot. For standard removals at established residential addresses, no permit is typically required — but we'll let you know if your situation needs one.

Eden Prairie Tree Removal Cost
