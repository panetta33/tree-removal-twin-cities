Heritage-Quality Tree Care for Edina Properties

Edina's neighborhoods grew up around their trees — and many of those trees are now older than the houses around them. The Country Club District's stately elm replacements (the originals are mostly gone to Dutch Elm Disease), Morningside's mature maples, Indian Hills' towering oaks, and the heritage canopy throughout the Edinborough and Edina Highlands neighborhoods all represent decades — sometimes a century — of growth that can't be replaced quickly.

That makes Edina tree work different. When something has to come down, it usually means navigating tight residential lots, working around expensive landscaping, coordinating with neighbors, and sometimes working with the city on heritage-tree paperwork. We do all of this every week. Tree Removal Twin Cities serves all of Edina — ZIPs 55410, 55416, 55424, 55435, 55436, 55439 — with vetted local crews and the equipment to do it right.

What's Different About Edina Tree Work

Tight residential lots. Many Edina lots, especially in older neighborhoods like Country Club and Morningside, are surprisingly tight given the size of the trees on them. A 70-foot maple in a 50-foot-wide lot, with a garage on one side and a neighbor's roof on the other, requires careful rigging and sectional takedown — not a chainsaw-and-hope approach.

Heritage and historic considerations. The Edina Country Club District is a National Register historic district, and tree management there carries extra sensitivity. Even outside historic districts, many homeowners rightly think of their trees as historic specimens worth caring for. We approach every job with that mindset.

Edina's tree ordinances. The City of Edina regulates removal of trees in the public right-of-way (boulevard trees) and on properties with significant heritage trees. Permits are sometimes required. We research and handle all permitting as part of the quote — you don't deal with City Hall.

EAB and oak wilt. Edina has been heavily impacted by Emerald Ash Borer; most private ash trees in the city are infested or dying. Oak wilt is also present in pockets and requires DNR-compliant disposal. We handle both.

A note on heritage tree preservation: if a healthy tree just needs structural pruning to be safe, our arborists will say so — we don't remove trees that can be saved. The arborists' code is "right tree, right place, right care." Removal is a last resort, not a first option.

What Does Tree Service Cost?

Honest answer: it depends. Tree work pricing varies dramatically based on tree size, condition, location, equipment needed, access, and the time of year. We don't list prices on this site because every job is unique and every provider sets their own rates.

What we can promise: the tree services we connect you with provide free, written estimates before any work begins. You'll know exactly what your job costs before agreeing to anything — and you're free to decline.

Pricing red flags: never pay cash up-front, never agree to verbal-only pricing, and never let work start without a written estimate. A reputable tree service will provide all three without hesitation.
