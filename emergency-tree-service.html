When Trees Don't Wait, Neither Do We

Minnesota storms don't politely wait until business hours. Microbursts, ice loads, snow loads, derechos, and just-plain-rotten old wood all bring trees down — sometimes on the worst possible thing. When that happens, you need a crew that's experienced, equipped, and actually answers the phone at 2 a.m.

We dispatch 24/7 across the Twin Cities for true tree emergencies. Most calls get an on-site assessment within hours.

What Counts as a Tree Emergency?

  • A tree on your house, garage, vehicle, fence, or shed
  • A large limb hanging in the canopy ("widow-maker") that could fall
  • A tree blocking your driveway or the street
  • A tree leaning suddenly after a storm
  • Trees down on or near power lines (call Xcel first — see below)
  • Visible cracks or splits in a major trunk or limb after high winds
⚠ Power line safety: If a tree is touching or near a power line, do not approach it. Call Xcel Energy at 1-800-895-1999 first and report it. They'll de-energize the line before any tree work can safely begin. We'll coordinate with the utility for you.

What to Do Before We Arrive

  • Stay away from the tree. Damaged trees can shift unpredictably.
  • Keep kids and pets indoors. Especially during wind.
  • Take photos. Wide and close-up shots help with your insurance claim.
  • Don't touch power lines or anything in contact with them. Even non-conductive-looking things can carry current.
  • Notify your insurance company. Most policies require prompt notice of damage.

Working With Your Homeowner's Insurance

Most homeowner's insurance policies cover emergency tree removal when a tree has damaged a covered structure (your home, garage, fence). Most professional tree services will provide:

  • Detailed itemized invoices that match adjuster expectations
  • Before/during/after photo documentation
  • Direct billing options for major insurers (ask when you call)
  • Help understanding what's typically covered vs. not

Emergency Pricing

Emergency tree work costs more than scheduled work — there's no way around it. Night crews, equipment mobilization, traffic control, and higher risk all factor in. That said, we don't engage in storm-chaser price gouging. Expect costs roughly 1.5×–2× normal removal pricing for after-hours emergency response.

We also offer a discount on the cleanup phase if you're willing to wait until the next business day for non-life-safety situations (e.g., a tree across your driveway but not on a structure).

Beware of Storm Chasers

After every major storm, out-of-state crews descend on the Twin Cities offering "great deals." Many are unlicensed, uninsured, and gone before any cleanup mistakes can be addressed. Always verify:
  • Local business address (not just a P.O. box)
  • Current Minnesota tree care license
  • Certificate of insurance — and call the agent to verify it's active
  • Reviews on Google with a history older than the storm
