FAQ

Common Questions

How much does tree removal cost in the Twin Cities?

Every job is different — cost depends on tree size, condition, location, and accessibility. We don't quote prices on this site because every property is different and every provider sets their own rates. The tree services we connect you with provide free, written estimates so you'll see exact pricing for your specific job before agreeing to anything.

How do I know the tree service is legitimate?

Always ask any tree service for a current certificate of insurance and a written estimate before work begins. A reputable company will provide both without hesitation. We recommend you verify the certificate directly with the listed insurance agent. Never hire a tree service that won't show proof of insurance.

Do you handle emergency tree removal?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency response across the Twin Cities for storm damage, fallen trees, and hazardous limbs. Call us anytime at (952) 260-9151.

Will the tree service work with my homeowner's insurance?

For storm or property damage claims, most local tree services will document the work with photos and provide detailed itemized invoices that insurance adjusters accept. Confirm this directly with your chosen provider.

Do you remove the stump too?

Stump grinding is a separate service that we recommend after most removals. We can bundle stump grinding with your tree removal for a discount — just mention it when requesting your quote.

What if my tree is close to power lines?

Trees near power lines require special techniques and sometimes coordination with Xcel Energy. We're experienced with these jobs and will handle the planning at no extra cost to you.
