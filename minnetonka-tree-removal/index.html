Professional Tree Removal Throughout Minnetonka

Minnetonka earned its reputation as one of the Twin Cities' most wooded suburbs the hard way — by holding onto its trees for generations. From the bluffs and ravines off Minnehaha Creek, to the heavy-canopy streets in Glen Lake and Tonka Bay, to the lakefront properties along Lake Minnetonka, this is a city defined by big, mature, often very old trees.

Beautiful, yes. But mature trees eventually need management — and the older a tree gets, the more skill, equipment, and insurance it takes to remove it safely. Why Minnetonka Trees Need More Attention Than Most

Emerald Ash Borer is decimating the ash population. Minnetonka has been actively treating selected high-value public ash trees, but the majority of private ash trees in the city are doomed. If you have an ash, it likely needs to come down within the next few years — and dead standing ash is among the most dangerous trees to remove because the wood becomes brittle and unpredictable. Don't wait.

Oak wilt is active in Minnetonka. Several pockets of oak wilt have been confirmed, and the disease spreads aggressively in our climate. Removing infected red oaks promptly — and disposing of the wood properly — is the only way to protect neighboring healthy oaks. We follow DNR oak wilt protocols, including timing-of-year restrictions.

The terrain. Many Minnetonka properties feature steep slopes, ravines, drop-offs, and wetlands — terrain that complicates rigging, equipment access, and debris removal. Our crews are equipped for this kind of work; many "tree services" aren't.

Working near Lake Minnetonka? Shoreline tree work can require coordination with the Minnehaha Creek Watershed District and sometimes the DNR (especially for trees overhanging water). We'll handle the permitting research for you.
