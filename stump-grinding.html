Stump Grinding & Removal in the Twin Cities

Get rid of that old stump for good — and reclaim your yard space.

Why Get Rid of the Stump?

Leftover stumps aren't just eyesores. They're tripping hazards, lawn-mowing obstacles, and magnets for carpenter ants, termites, fungi, and even mice. Many also send up suckers — new shoots that turn into a thorny thicket if you don't keep on top of them. Grinding the stump out solves all of these problems at once.

Grinding vs. Full Stump Removal

There are two ways to get rid of a stump:

  • Stump grinding uses a specialized machine that chews the stump down 6–12 inches below grade. We then backfill with the resulting wood chips (or topsoil if you prefer) and you can lay sod or plant grass right over the spot. This is what most homeowners want — it's faster, cheaper, and much less invasive to your yard.
  • Full stump removal means digging out the entire root ball. It's necessary for some construction or when you want to plant a new tree in the exact same spot, but it leaves a much bigger hole and costs significantly more.
Tip: If we're already on-site for a tree removal, adding stump grinding is a fraction of what it costs to schedule separately. Bundle them when you request your quote.

Our Stump Grinding Process

  • Locate utilities. We call 811 to mark buried lines before grinding — never skip this step.
  • Set up protection. We use a shield to control flying chips and protect windows, siding, and vehicles.
  • Grind 6–12 inches below grade. Deep enough for lawn or shallow landscaping.
  • Surface roots. We chase major surface roots out from the stump.
  • Cleanup. Chips backfilled into the hole, or hauled away and replaced with topsoil if you prefer.

Stump Grinding Cost in the Twin Cities

Stump grinding is typically priced by stump diameter (measured at ground level). Approximate Twin Cities ranges:

  • Small stump (under 12"): $75 – $150
  • Medium (12"–24"): $150 – $300
  • Large (24"–36"): $300 – $500
  • Very large (36"+): $500+ / custom quote

Multiple stumps in one visit get a bundle rate. There's often a minimum trip charge ($150–$200) if it's a one-stump job in a far-out part of the metro.

What About the Wood Chips?

Standard practice is to push the chips back into the hole. They're great as walkway mulch or for filling low spots elsewhere in your yard. If you want them hauled away and the spot filled with topsoil instead, just say so — it's a small upcharge.
