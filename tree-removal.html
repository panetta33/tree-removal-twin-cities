Tree Removal Services in the Twin Cities

Safe, efficient removal of dead, dying, damaged, or unwanted trees — large or small, easy access or tight quarters.

Professional Tree Removal You Can Trust

Removing a tree — especially a large or hazardous one — is one of the most dangerous jobs in residential property work. It takes the right equipment, the right training, and the right insurance. Our local crews bring all three to every job, from a single dead boxelder in the backyard to a 70-foot silver maple leaning toward your roof.

When to Remove a Tree

You may need tree removal if:

  • The tree is dead, dying, or has extensive decay
  • It's leaning dangerously toward your home, garage, or power lines
  • It has been damaged by storm, wind, or ice and is now unsafe
  • Roots are damaging your foundation, sewer lines, or driveway
  • It's infected with Dutch Elm Disease, Emerald Ash Borer, oak wilt, or another fatal pathogen
  • It's simply in the wrong spot — too close to a structure or shading where you don't want shade
Not sure if you need removal? Sometimes a tree that looks dangerous can be saved with pruning or cabling. Our local arborists give honest assessments — if the tree can be saved, we'll tell you.

Our Tree Removal Process

Every removal follows the same proven process:

  • Free on-site estimate. We come look at the tree, evaluate access, hazards, and surrounding obstacles, and give you a written quote.
  • Permit & utility check. If your tree is on a boulevard or near power lines, we handle locates and any city permits.
  • Safe controlled takedown. Depending on the situation we rope-rig sections down, use a bucket truck or crane, or fell the tree if there's open space.
  • Cleanup. We chip the brush, haul off the logs (or stack them for firewood if you want), and rake the area clean.
  • Optional stump grinding. Most customers add stump grinding — bundled it's significantly cheaper than booking it separately.

Common Trees We Remove in the Twin Cities

Minnesota has its own particular tree-removal challenges. We regularly remove:

  • Ash trees — most are now infected with Emerald Ash Borer and need to come down
  • Elms — Dutch Elm Disease remains widespread; infected elms must be removed and properly disposed of
  • Oaks — oak wilt outbreaks require careful timing and disposal
  • Silver maples & boxelders — fast-growing but weak-wooded; common storm-damage casualties
  • Pines & spruces — often planted too close to homes decades ago

Tree Removal Cost in the Twin Cities

Pricing depends on the tree's size, condition, location, and access. Typical Twin Cities ranges:

  • Small tree (under 25 ft): $300 – $700
  • Medium tree (25–60 ft): $700 – $1,500
  • Large tree (60+ ft): $1,500 – $3,000+
  • Crane-assisted or high-hazard: custom quote

These ranges include cleanup. Stump grinding and emergency rates are priced separately. Always request a written quote and avoid anyone who insists on cash-only or won't show proof of insurance.

Why Insurance Matters

If an uninsured tree service drops a tree on your house, your homeowner's insurance pays — and your premiums go up. Every crew we dispatch carries general liability and workers' comp insurance. You can request a certificate of insurance before any work begins.
